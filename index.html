RESEARCH

Engineering at the intersection of mechanics and robotics.

My research focuses on adaptive robotic systems that change their physical properties or morphology to operate effectively across different environments and tasks.

I am especially interested in mechanical design, robotic mechanisms, experimental validation, prototyping, and the integration of complete robotic systems.

ABOUT

Mechanical engineer. Researcher. Builder.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Yale University and an NSF Graduate Research Fellow. Before Yale, I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of California, Irvine.

My research experience includes robotics laboratories at Yale, UC Irvine, UC Santa Barbara, and UC San Diego, with work spanning adaptive robots, soft robotics, assistive devices, and bioinspired systems.
